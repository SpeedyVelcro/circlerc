v1.1.0 contains a couple features and improvements that I felt were
important to bring CircleRC up to a more polished standard.

## Changelog
- Victory screen appears when you complete a level, comparing the time
  you just achieved and your best time, and allowing you to retry the
  level.
- Big enemy car refactor that brings with it a slew of improvements
  - Enemy cars try to space themselves out when they share a path. No
    more sitting in front of a convoy to bunch them all up together.
  - Enemy cars will now damage you if they crash into you. Previously
    you would only take damage if you crashed into them, but now it
    works both ways.
  - Enemy cars are less likely to get stuck. When they crash they back
    up a bit before they try to continue along their path.
  - It's harder for the player to get stuck on enemy cars, since they
    now respect physics and you can push them.
- Pip collection sound modified to sound brighter
- Fixed bug where you could die after completing the level. Now you
  gain invincibility as soon as the flag gets circled.
- Version number added to main menu
- License display system on credits menu.
